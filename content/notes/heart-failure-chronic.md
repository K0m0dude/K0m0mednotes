background: |+
  **Heart Failure is inability of the heart to pump oxygenated blood to metabolising tissue despite an adequate filling pressure.**

  It can be categorized into **Acute** or **Chronic** depending on development of the condition overtime. (Acute: Minutes, Hours, Days; Chronic: Months, Years)

  Other types of HF allows for more etiological categorization such as:


  | Type | Explanation | Signs | Typical Causes |
  | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
  | Systolic HF (HFrEF) | Inability of the ventricle to contract normally during systole | ↓ Cardiac output, EF <40% | IHD, MI, cardiomyopathy |
  | Diastolic HF (HFpEF) | Inability of the ventricle to relax and fill normally during diastole | ↑ Filling pressures, EF >50% (preserved EF) | LVH, constrictive pericarditis, tamponade, restrictive cardiomyopathy, obesity |
  | LVF | Failure of the left ventricle to adequately pump blood into the systemic circulation | Pulmonary congestion: dyspnoea, orthopnoea, PND, pulmonary oedema; low-output symptoms: fatigue, poor exercise tolerance | IHD, hypertension, valvular disease, cardiomyopathy |
  | RVF | Failure of the right ventricle to adequately pump blood into the pulmonary circulation | Systemic venous congestion: peripheral oedema, ascites, raised JVP, hepatomegaly | LVF, pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary stenosis, chronic lung disease |
  | Low-output HF | Cardiac output is reduced and fails to increase appropriately with exertion | Fatigue, poor exercise tolerance, peripheral hypoperfusion | Pump failure, excessive preload, excessive afterload |
  | High-output HF | Cardiac output is normal or increased but insufficient to meet abnormally high metabolic demands | Initially RVF features, later LVF features | Anaemia, pregnancy, hyperthyroidism, AVM, Paget's disease, beriberi |

diagnosis: |+
  Diagnosis of HF depends on the symptoms presented:

  If relevant symptoms are present:

  - ECGs → possible causes for HF e.g. Atrial Fib.
  - NT-proBNP (>400 ng/litre) levels

  are tested before undergoing Echocardiogram for assessment of **valves**, **ventricles**...

  - [NICE Guideline on Chronic HF](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng106/chapter/recommendations)


  | Heart Failure Type | Symptoms | Signs |
  | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | **Systolic Heart Failure (HFrEF)** | Fatigue, poor exercise tolerance | Reduced EF (<40%), reduced cardiac output |
  | **Diastolic Heart Failure (HFpEF)** | Exertional dyspnoea, fatigue | Preserved EF (>50%), raised filling pressures |
  | **Left Ventricular Failure (LVF)** | Dyspnoea, poor exercise tolerance, fatigue, orthopnoea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnoea (PND), nocturnal cough, wheeze ("cardiac asthma"), nocturia | Pink frothy sputum, cold peripheries, weight loss |
  | **Right Ventricular Failure (RVF)** | Nausea, anorexia | Peripheral oedema (legs/thighs/sacrum/abdominal wall), ascites, facial engorgement, epistaxis |
  | **Congestive Cardiac Failure (CCF)** | Combination of LVF and RVF symptoms | Combination of LVF and RVF signs |
  | **Low-Output Heart Failure** | Fatigue, poor exercise tolerance | Reduced cardiac output, cold peripheries, poor peripheral perfusion |
  | **High-Output Heart Failure** | Symptoms of RVF initially, later symptoms of LVF | Signs of RVF initially (oedema, ascites), later signs of LVF |
